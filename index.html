Our story

A Marshall table
built on welcome.

For a quarter century, Mariachi Fiesta has welcomed Marshall with warm plates, house salsa, generous margaritas, and room at the table.

Owner Eloy Cid carries that tradition forward with a simple focus: familiar Mexican favorites, attentive service, and the kind of welcome that turns a first visit into a tradition.

Eloy is often the face guests meet at the bar or around the dining room. Come hungry, settle into a booth, and let him and the team take care of the rest.
